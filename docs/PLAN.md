# Equarian Markets: becoming Oracular Macro

From the portfolio plan of 1 November 2026, which decided which apps to grow and charge for. Markets is not one of them on its own: it becomes the **Macro** tab of [Oracular](https://gitlab.com/eatentam/oracular) (see its `docs/PLAN.md`). The final call on the fold, and on pointing markets.ethantam.dev at Oracular, is Ethan's; it is listed there as a decision only he can make.

## Why

- One brand is cheaper to grow than two. Oracular is the flagship, and this dashboard is its top-down half.
- Futures traders are a feeder into Oracular. Two of the three AI reviews put it that way; the user panel's day trader said he would pay about US$25 a month once the bias forecast shows its record.
- The honest record is the proof that sells the tab. Keep every "doesn't beat a plain average" line as it is.

## What moves

The Risk Appetite Index, the growth × inflation regime map, the absorption ratio, the NY Fed recession probability, flows and premia (CTA positioning, the variance risk premium, the stock–bond correlation, the calendar), the SPX option flow, the ES/NQ market bias with its walk-forward track record, and the alt signals (which Oracular's worker already feeds). The 45 indicator cards come too, in the same five groups.

| | Free in Oracular | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Risk Appetite score | yes | yes |
| Everything else above | no | yes |

A Pro tier for futures traders may follow later, if the Macro tab pulls them in. Markets never gave buy or sell calls and won't inside Oracular either.

## When

- **December 2026 to January 2027:** build the Macro tab in Oracular, before its Gate 1 review on 29 January 2027.
- **Once the tab ships:** markets.ethantam.dev redirects to it. Until then this site stays live as it is.
- **Until then, this repo gets fixes only**, no new features. The scope table in `README.md` (Markets vs Oracular) still holds for anything that lands before the move.

## Data on a paid tier

FRED, Treasury.gov, the Fed, the BOJ and the CFTC are public sources and stay, with credit. Yahoo and CBOE are free and unofficial feeds whose terms do not allow display in a paid product; on Oracular's Premium tier they are replaced by licensed data (quotes are requested in November 2026) or kept to the free tier. The live-data-only rule carries over unchanged: a missing feed shows as missing, never as a placeholder.
