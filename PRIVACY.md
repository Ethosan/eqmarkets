# Equarian Markets: Privacy

Equarian Markets is a personal project by Ethan Tam. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. This page says what data touches the service and where it goes.

## What is collected

- **Nothing stored about you.** Equarian Markets doesn't ask for your name, email, or any sign-in. There are no cookies set for tracking.
- **This device.** As an installable app (PWA), the last good market data is saved on your device so the dashboard opens instantly and still shows the last values offline. Theme preferences and dismissed notices are also saved locally. Anyone who uses that browser can see this copy. Clearing your browser data removes it.

## AI explanations (optional)

- The **Explain in plain English** button sends only the indicator numbers shown on the page (never text you type) to a Mistral AI model through a Cloudflare Pages Function. The Function does not log or store the request.

## Who else processes data

- **Cloudflare:** hosts the site and the serverless function.
- **Yahoo Finance, FRED (St. Louis Fed), Bank of Japan, CFTC:** provide the market data.
- **Mistral AI:** only if you tap "Explain in plain English."

## What is not done

- No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cross-site tracking.
- No selling or sharing of personal data (there isn't any to sell).
- No account system — there is nothing to delete because nothing is stored about you server-side.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before it takes effect.
