# Equarian Markets: Terms

These terms cover your use of Equarian Markets, a personal project by Ethan Tam. By using the app you agree to them.

## Not financial advice

Equarian Markets is a context tool for reading macro markets. Everything on the page — the Risk Appetite Index, regime maps, absorption ratio, recession probability, market-bias forecasts, CTA positioning estimates, option-flow panels, and AI explanations — is computed from public market data and can be wrong. **Nothing in Equarian Markets is financial, investment, trading, tax, or legal advice.** Don't make trading or investment decisions based on it. Always do your own research and consult a qualified professional.

## The models can be wrong

Every model on the page is a simplification. The Risk Appetite Index, the growth × inflation regime, the absorption ratio, the NY Fed recession probability, the market-bias forecast, and the CTA positioning estimate all use historical data and statistical methods that have known limitations. Backtested performance is not a guarantee of future results. The option-flow panel estimates dealer positioning from public option board data and makes simplifying assumptions.

## The AI can be wrong

The "Explain in plain English" feature is powered by Mistral AI. It can hallucinate, misread numbers, and give confident-sounding answers that are wrong. Treat every explanation as a second opinion, not a conclusion.

## The data can be wrong

Market data comes from Yahoo Finance, FRED, the Bank of Japan, and the CFTC. Data can be delayed, revised, or incorrect. All indicators inherit any errors in their source data. Verify anything that matters against the original source.
